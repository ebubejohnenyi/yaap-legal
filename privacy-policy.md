
# Privacy Policy

# Last updated: August 2026

## About YAPP

YAPP is a WhatsApp-native food ordering platform that connects 
customers with restaurants and delivery riders in Lagos, Nigeria. 
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect 
your personal information when you use our service.

## Information We Collect

**When you order food via WhatsApp:**
- Your WhatsApp phone number
- Your name (when provided)
- Your delivery address and location
- Your order history and food preferences
- Your device location (when you share it)

**When you register as a vendor or rider:**
- Your full name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your restaurant name and address (vendors)
- Your vehicle type (riders)

**Payment information:**
- We do not store your card details
- Payments are processed securely by Paystack
- We store only transaction references and payment status

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Process and deliver your food orders
- Match you with nearby restaurants and riders
- Send you order updates and delivery notifications via WhatsApp
- Remember your delivery address and food preferences
- Process payments through Paystack
- Improve our service and resolve disputes
- Comply with legal obligations

## Who We Share Your Information With

**Restaurants (Vendors):**
We share your order details and delivery address with the 
restaurant fulfilling your order so they can prepare your food.

**Riders (Dispatchers):**
We share your delivery address and contact information with 
the rider assigned to deliver your order.

**Paystack:**
Payment processing is handled by Paystack. Your payment 
information is subject to Paystack's Privacy Policy at 
paystack.com/privacy.

**We do not sell your personal data to third parties.**

## Data Retention

- Order history is retained for financial and legal records
- Your profile and preferences are kept while your account 
  is active
- You may request deletion of your account at any time
- After account deletion, order history is retained for 
  a minimum of 7 years for financial compliance

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your account and personal data
- Withdraw consent at any time by contacting us

## Data Security

We use industry standard security measures including:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
- Supabase secure database hosting
- Row-level security policies
- Restricted access to personal data

## Children's Privacy

YAPP is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do 
not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will 
notify users of significant changes via WhatsApp message.
