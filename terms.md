**Payment:**

- A delivery fee is added to every order
- Payment is processed securely by Paystack
- Accepted payment methods include card, bank transfer, 
  and USSD

  case-by-case basis and processed within 3-5 business days

**Delivery:**
- Delivery times are estimates and may vary based on

  receive your order

- YAPP is not responsible for orders that cannot be 
  delivered due to an incorrect address provided by 
  the customer

**Ratings:**
- After delivery you will be asked to rate your experience
- Ratings help us maintain quality on the platform
- Low ratings trigger a review by our operations team
